# Rasphia Platform Overview

Rasphia is a commerce platform built for both modern merchants and modern shoppers. It combines AI-assisted discovery, merchant storefront tooling, conversational commerce, checkout, payments, invoices, and post-purchase workflows into one system.

This document explains:

- What Rasphia offers merchants
- What Rasphia offers users
- Why Rasphia is better than a traditional shopping experience

## Rasphia for Merchants

### 1. Merchant onboarding and storefront setup

Merchants can join Rasphia and create a branded digital storefront with their own identity, catalog, and payment setup.

Core capabilities:

- Merchant registration and onboarding
- Storefront configuration and slug-based public storefronts
- Merchant profile management
- Brand presentation with merchant-specific store pages
- Merchant-specific payment configuration
- Razorpay setup as the primary payment option
- Optional Seedhape integration support for compatible flows

### 2. Product catalog management

Rasphia gives merchants direct control over their inventory and merchandising.

Core capabilities:

- Add, edit, and remove products
- Upload product images
- Bulk product upload support
- Product metadata management
- Product availability controls
- Stock quantity tracking
- Merchant-specific product ownership mapping

This allows merchants to maintain a live catalog without relying on a third-party marketplace operator to update listings for them.

### 3. Storefront commerce and direct selling

Each merchant can sell through their Rasphia storefront without depending only on large marketplace platforms.

Core capabilities:

- Public storefront pages for discovery and purchase
- Merchant-linked checkout flows
- Product-level purchase initiation
- Single-merchant grouped ordering
- Integrated payment flow
- Customer order creation and status tracking

This gives merchants more control over branding, customer experience, and repeat engagement.

### 4. Payment configuration and verification

Rasphia supports merchant-managed payment setup with operational safeguards.

Core capabilities:

- Razorpay configuration for merchant payments
- Razorpay checkout as the default payment flow
- Payment verification flow
- Merchant-specific payment credentials storage
- Checkout support across web and WhatsApp-assisted flows

This reduces friction for merchants who want a clean setup without building their own payment stack.

### 5. Internal invoice generation

Rasphia can generate invoices internally instead of forcing merchants to depend on an external invoicing workflow.

Core capabilities:

- Internal invoice generation after payment verification
- GST-aware invoice structure
- PDF invoice generation
- Invoice file storage
- Invoice email sending to customers
- Merchant invoice visibility from the dashboard
- Invoice resend support

This helps merchants keep invoicing closer to the order system, making operations simpler and more auditable.

### 6. Merchant order management

Rasphia gives merchants a practical view of orders and post-purchase operations.

Core capabilities:

- Merchant dashboard for orders
- Expanded order details view
- Order status updates
- Active order monitoring
- Service request visibility
- CSV export for orders
- Invoice visibility per order

This makes merchant operations more manageable than juggling spreadsheets, payment screenshots, and chat messages.

### 7. Service requests and post-purchase workflows

Rasphia supports structured handling of customer issues after purchase.

Core capabilities:

- Refund requests
- Replacement requests
- Cancellation requests
- Merchant-side visibility into service requests
- Status-based request eligibility
- Request timelines and snapshots

This creates a more reliable support workflow than handling everything ad hoc over DMs.

### 8. Merchant analytics and business insights

Rasphia gives merchants access to operational and business intelligence, including via conversational interfaces.

Core capabilities:

- Sales tracking
- Order summaries
- Best-selling item analysis
- Restock visibility
- Cart activity visibility
- Time-based performance questions such as:
  - total sales today
  - total sales last month
  - best-selling items
  - items that need restocking
  - users with active carts

This helps merchants use Rasphia as an operating layer, not just a catalog page.

### 9. WhatsApp commerce for merchants

Rasphia extends merchant operations into WhatsApp so merchants can manage parts of commerce conversationally.

Core capabilities:

- Merchant onboarding from WhatsApp
- Product upload and updates via WhatsApp
- Stock updates via WhatsApp
- Active order lookup via WhatsApp
- Order status updates via WhatsApp
- Merchant analytics queries via WhatsApp
- Merchant-aware customer chat context

This reduces the gap between where merchants already work and where sales operations happen.

## Rasphia for Users

### 1. AI-assisted discovery

Rasphia is not just a search box. It is designed to help users discover products that fit their preferences, intent, and context.

Core capabilities:

- Conversational discovery
- AI-guided product recommendations
- Persona-aware product matching
- Curated and contextual discovery experiences
- Multi-merchant exploration

This is especially useful for categories where taste, style, preference, or suitability matters.

### 2. User profile and personalization

Rasphia builds shopping around the user rather than treating every shopper the same.

Core capabilities:

- User profile management
- Persona creation and updates
- Saved customer details
- Saved address handling
- Preference-driven experiences

This allows the platform to move from generic browsing to more relevant commerce.

### 3. Modern checkout experience

Rasphia aims to make checkout feel closer to a guided buying flow than a generic payment form.

Core capabilities:

- Web checkout flow
- Razorpay-first checkout
- Grouped cart-to-order flow for the same merchant
- Customer detail prefill from verified profile data
- Saved address selection
- Quantity-aware checkout
- Multi-item purchase handling
- Payment verification support

This reduces drop-off caused by repetitive forms and fragmented payment handling.

### 4. Persistent cart and order continuity

Users can continue their journey without losing context.

Core capabilities:

- Cart persistence in the database
- Merchant-grouped checkout handling
- Cart cleanup after successful payment verification
- Resume-where-you-left-off purchase flow

This is much better than losing a shopping session when a browser tab closes or a chat flow gets interrupted.

### 5. Orders, invoices, and post-purchase visibility

Users can see what they bought and what happened next.

Core capabilities:

- Order history
- Latest orders view
- Paginated order querying
- Order status visibility
- Invoice generation visibility
- Invoice delivery by email
- Invoice PDF access
- Structured support request initiation

This gives users more confidence after purchase and reduces uncertainty around payment success and fulfillment.

### 6. WhatsApp shopping experience

Rasphia brings a real shopping flow into WhatsApp instead of reducing WhatsApp to a support-only channel.

Core capabilities:

- User registration via WhatsApp
- Product discovery via WhatsApp
- Merchant-specific shopping context via WhatsApp
- Order creation assistance via WhatsApp
- Payment confirmation flow via WhatsApp
- Latest order querying via WhatsApp
- Refund, replacement, and cancellation requests via WhatsApp
- Final status messaging and invoice messaging after payment

This is useful for users who are more comfortable buying through chat than through traditional websites.

### 7. Wishlist and preference memory

Rasphia supports ongoing shopping intent rather than one-off transactions only.

Core capabilities:

- Wishlist add/remove/view
- Preference memory across sessions
- Better future discovery based on prior context

This makes the platform feel more like a shopping assistant and less like a static catalog.

## Why Rasphia Is Better Than a Traditional Shopping Experience

Traditional shopping systems, especially large generic marketplaces, are usually optimized for listings, transactions, and ads. Rasphia is optimized for relevance, guidance, merchant control, and conversational commerce.

### 1. Discovery is guided, not manual

Traditional shopping:

- Users scroll endlessly
- Search depends on exact keywords
- Product discovery is often noisy and repetitive

Rasphia:

- Helps users discover through conversation
- Uses user context and preferences
- Surfaces more relevant products faster

### 2. The experience is personalized, not generic

Traditional shopping:

- Every user sees broadly similar listing structures
- Context is shallow
- Preferences are rarely carried through meaningfully

Rasphia:

- Uses persona and profile context
- Supports preference-aware curation
- Makes future interactions smarter over time

### 3. Merchants have more control

Traditional shopping:

- Merchants often surrender branding and customer experience
- Marketplace rules dominate the buying journey
- Sellers depend heavily on platform commissions and ranking systems

Rasphia:

- Gives merchants their own storefront presence
- Lets merchants manage payment setup and catalog directly
- Creates a more direct merchant-customer relationship

### 4. Commerce can happen where users already are

Traditional shopping:

- The experience is usually limited to websites or apps
- Chat channels are often disconnected from checkout

Rasphia:

- Extends commerce into WhatsApp
- Supports discovery, order flow, payment follow-up, and support in chat
- Keeps website and conversational commerce more aligned

### 5. Operations are more integrated

Traditional shopping:

- Payments, invoices, customer support, order tracking, and follow-ups are often split across tools

Rasphia:

- Connects checkout, payment verification, invoices, order visibility, and service requests
- Helps merchants operate from a more unified system

### 6. The platform is more useful for independent and modern merchants

Traditional shopping:

- Large marketplaces often favor scale, paid acquisition, and platform dependency

Rasphia:

- Works well for independent brands, founder-led stores, and conversational sellers
- Gives smaller merchants tooling that usually requires multiple products stitched together

## Summary

Rasphia is more than an online storefront builder and more than an AI shopping assistant. It is a full commerce layer that connects:

- AI-powered discovery
- Merchant-owned storefronts
- Conversational shopping
- Razorpay-based payments
- Internal invoice generation
- Order management
- Post-purchase support
- Merchant analytics

For merchants, Rasphia offers control, automation, and a more direct relationship with customers.

For users, Rasphia offers a more intuitive, personalized, and guided buying experience.

Compared with traditional shopping, Rasphia is better because it reduces friction, improves relevance, unifies workflows, and makes commerce feel more human.
